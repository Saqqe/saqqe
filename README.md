# 💫 About Me:
I am an Engineering Manager specializing in Service Delivery and Technical Pre-sales, currently based in Stockholm. With a robust background in software engineering and systems architecture, I lead and mentor teams of delivery managers, ensuring efficient project execution and exceptional service quality.<br><br>My career trajectory includes roles such as Interim Consultant Manager, Technical and Delivery Leader, and Product Owner/System Architect at Knowit Connectivity. In these positions, I have successfully managed global, cross-functional teams, translating complex requirements into clear, actionable strategies. My approach combines agile leadership with a deep understanding of technology, consistently exceeding client expectations and delivering impactful results.<br><br>I hold a Bachelor in Electrical Engineering from KTH Royal Institute of Technology and have obtained certifications including Certified SAFe 6 Product Owner/Product Manager, IASA CITA Foundation, and Certified Scrum Master. My technical expertise spans cloud to embedded software architecture, and I am proficient in technologies such as Kubernetes, Docker, C/C++, Python, Go, Rust, and AWS.
